Warp &
Weft

Every term in the GPU Glossary, from the silicon up, drawn out and made to move.

Warp & Weft is an independent, unofficial companion to Modal's GPU Glossary. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Modal or NVIDIA.

The glossary's text is © Modal Labs and its contributors and is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (source on GitHub). This site adapts that material: entries are rewritten and expanded, code samples are reproduced, and every figure is redrawn as an interactive illustration. Notes marked “Beyond the glossary” add context that is not in the original; notes marked “Checked the math” flag places where we recomputed a figure.

Why the name? Warps, the 32-thread groups GPUs schedule together, are named after weaving, “the first parallel thread technology” (Lindholm et al., 2008). The weft is what runs across them.
